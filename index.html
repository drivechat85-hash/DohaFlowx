About DohaFlow

DohaFlow is a student-built prototype for the MES Youth Hackathon 2026. It tackles one daily problem: hundreds of cars reaching one school gate in the same ten minutes.

The problem

Most school-run cars carry one child and arrive together. That creates queues on nearby roads, wastes fuel, and cuts into family mornings.

Our approach

Two simple levers: put neighbours in the same car, and spread arrivals across short windows. Both work without new roads or buses.

Safety first

Only parents verified through the school portal appear in matches. Children’s names and exact addresses are never shown, only the area and approximate distance.

Prototype notice

Families, distances, and queue levels here are sample data. Nothing is sent to a server; your choices stay in this browser.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Yes. DohaFlow doesn’t handle payments; families agree on fuel sharing between themselves.

How are families verified?

In the full version, parents sign in with their school portal credentials. Unverified profiles are hidden unless you turn the filter off.

What if my child’s school isn’t listed?

The prototype covers four schools. Adding a school only needs its gate windows and capacity.

How does the match score work?

It combines how close the family is, how near your drop-off times are, and whether the children share a grade band.

Can I cancel a gate slot?

Yes. Open Gate slots and choose Cancel reservation to free the space for another family.
